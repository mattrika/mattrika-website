Privacy Policy

How Mattrika Technologies collects, protects, and handles personal data and confidential client technical information.

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Mattrika Technologies ("Mattrika", "we", "us", or "our"), respecting your privacy and securing your proprietary data is foundational to how we operate. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, storage, and processing when you browse our website (mattrika.com) or contract our engineering services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide software development, design, and technical consultation:

  • Contact Details: Your name, company name, corporate email address, and phone number when you submit an inquiry or project request.
  • Project Specifications: Technical specifications, architecture goals, repository access, and documents shared during discovery.
  • Technical Usage Data: Anonymized metrics including IP address, browser type, device details, and interaction logs to optimize site performance and security.

3. How We Use Information

We utilize collected information solely for legitimate business and engineering purposes:

  • To evaluate project scopes and deliver architectural and timeline estimates.
  • To fulfill contractual engineering, design, and deployment deliverables.
  • To invoice services, maintain project communications, and provide technical support.
  • To ensure cyber-defense, monitor system reliability, and prevent fraudulent activity.

4. Zero Data Selling & Third-Party Processors

We never sell, rent, or trade client personal or technical information to third parties.

We only engage SOC-compliant, industry-standard third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., enterprise cloud hosts, secure payment gateways, and encrypted communication channels) strictly necessary to deliver our services, under rigorous confidentiality and data processing agreements.

5. Security & Data Retention

We employ modern defense-in-depth security measures including end-to-end TLS encryption in transit, strict RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) for code repositories, encrypted credential vaults, and multi-factor authentication across all developer tooling.

Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill project requirements, legal obligations, and tax reporting requirements, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have statutory rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request erasure ("right to be forgotten") of your personal data.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Privacy team at info@mattrika.com.

7. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses only essential and performance cookies to maintain session security and collect aggregated, privacy-preserving analytical metrics. You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time.
